A matter of public record

You would not be the first to change a letter.

Across Hindi, Bengali and Marathi cinema, adjusting a spelling on a numerologist's advice is common enough to be unremarkable.

Hindi cinema
Ajay Devgan
Ajay Devgn
Dropped the “a”. Ten letters became nine.
Hindi cinema
Tushar Kapoor
Tusshar Kapoor
A doubled consonant — the commonest correction of all.
Bengali cinema

Rani Mukerji
Lost an “h”, and the double “e” became a single “i”.
Marathi cinema
Swapnil Joshi
Swwapnil Joshi
A doubled “w” — the spelling he brands himself with.
Hindi cinema
Rajkumar Yadav
Rajkummar Rao
A doubled “m”, and a new surname to go with it.

Did the change make the difference? Nobody can prove that, and we will not claim it. What it shows is that the question gets taken seriously by people with a lot to lose.
